## 5. Renewal

This agreement automatically renews for one additional one-year term unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the renewal date.

The next renewal date is August 15, 2026. The notice deadline for cancellation or non-renewal is July 16, 2026.

## 6. Termination

Buyer may terminate this agreement for convenience after the current term by providing timely notice of non-renewal. Buyer may also terminate for uncured material breach after 15 days' written notice.

No termination fee applies after the current term.
