## 1. Parties

This Workplace Supplies Subscription Agreement is entered into by Example Buyer Operations Co. ("Buyer") and Brightline Office Supplies LLC ("Supplier"), a fictional supplier located at 1200 Sample Market Street, Suite 400, Portland, OR 97205.

- Agreement status: Completed
- Business owner: Procurement Ops
- Currency: USD
- Annual agreement value: USD 82,000
- Initial term: October 15, 2025 through October 14, 2026

## 3. Services

Supplier will provide recurring workplace supplies, catalog ordering access, and quarterly usage reports for Buyer's fictional office locations.

## 4. Fees

Buyer will pay Supplier an annual subscription and fulfillment fee of USD 82,000, invoiced quarterly in advance. Taxes, freight surcharges, and special-order items are excluded from the annual fee unless approved in writing.

## 5. Renewal

This agreement automatically renews for successive one-year renewal terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the renewal date.

The next renewal date is October 15, 2026. The required notice deadline for non-renewal is August 16, 2026.

## 6. Termination

Buyer may terminate this agreement only for Supplier's uncured material breach after 30 days' written notice. The agreement does not grant Buyer a right to terminate for convenience.

If the agreement is terminated for Supplier's uncured breach, no early termination fee applies. Termination for convenience is not available.
